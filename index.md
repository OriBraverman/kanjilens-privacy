# Privacy Policy for KanjiLens: AI Japanese Tutor

**Last Updated: November 14, 2026**

## Overview

KanjiLens ("the App") is a Japanese kanji learning application that recognizes kanji with on-device AI. The App has no user accounts, no ads, and no analytics, and the developer does not collect or receive any of your data.

## What Stays on Your Device

- **Camera images**: When you scan, the photo is analyzed on your device and is not uploaded, shared, or saved to your photo gallery.
- **Drawings**: Your handwritten kanji are evaluated on your device and are never transmitted.
- **Learning progress**: Your kanji collection, scores, streak, XP, and achievements are stored in the App's private storage on your device.

## What the Developer Collects

Nothing. The App does not ask for your name, email, location, or any other personal information, and it does not send your images, drawings, or progress to the developer or to any server operated by the developer.

## Third-Party Components

The Scan feature uses **Google ML Kit Text Recognition**, which runs on your device. ML Kit does not send your images to Google. Google may, however, collect limited technical data from ML Kit (such as device and app information, per-installation identifiers, performance metrics, and error codes) for diagnostics and usage analytics, as described in Google's [ML Kit data disclosure](https://developers.google.com/ml-kit/android-data-disclosure) and [Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

The App contains no advertising or tracking SDKs.

## Camera Permission

The App requests camera access only to scan kanji. The camera is used only while the Scan screen is open and never runs in the background. The App requests no other sensitive permissions (no microphone, location, contacts, or storage access).

## Data Storage and Deletion

Your progress is stored only in the App's private storage. If Android backup is enabled on your device, the operating system may include this data in your own device backup. You can delete all App data at any time by clearing the App's storage in Android settings or by uninstalling the App.

## Children's Privacy

KanjiLens is suitable for all ages. The developer does not knowingly collect any information from children or from anyone else.

## Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. Changes will be reflected in the "Last Updated" date above.
